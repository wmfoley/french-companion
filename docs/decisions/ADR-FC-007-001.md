# ADR-FC-007-001 — Preparation contracts and evidence

Status: **Draft for product-owner review**. FC-007A implements contract validation only.
Date: 2026-11-15

## Context and approved direction

FC-006 is accepted at 0248cfbbc19f4ed0b797600055e53fda99f9ca0f. Initial intake targets mostly printed French Immersion vocabulary lists and term-definition tables. Clearly disclosed partial study sets are permitted. Typical supported-sheet parent active preparation time should approach 120 seconds; this is a future measured target, not a result demonstrated by synthetic fixtures. Generated-question inspection remains optional.

## Preparation boundary

Use a separate version-1 evidence/preparation contract, not new properties on assignment schema v1. Capture identity/revision and preparation identity/revision are distinct from assignment contentRevision and document writeRevision. Preparation describes potential supported content; it never creates approval evidence, changes source fidelity, authenticates a parent or authorizes student practice. Existing FC-005 detailed and risk-acknowledged approval and FC-006 generation remain unchanged.

Evidence consists of exact, unnormalized source text, bounded regions, and candidate field spans. Vocabulary pairing must declare a labelled-pair or table-row evidence region containing both fields; array position is never an association. This checks internal consistency, not whether an OCR engine read a real worksheet correctly. Source-derived versus supplementary candidates remain distinct; neither classification proves teacher authenticity. Future conversion must preserve original excerpts and field provenance, mark unverified extraction accordingly, and exclude supplementary material from teacher-assigned practice.

## Outcomes and trust

Derive supported, partial or unsupported suitability from mechanically usable vocabulary pairs and coverage exclusions. Non-vocabulary regions remain disclosed rather than converted into invented vocabulary. Report reliability and content safety separately, including unknown states. Numeric confidence is optional and informational: absent/null remains unknown, never certainty. No calibrated confidence threshold or safety classifier is introduced. Explicit sufficient reliability and clear safety declarations are necessary for contract readiness, but these untrusted declarations are **not** production security evidence.

Partial results require disclosure of excluded candidates, unsupported/unresolved regions and fixed-code issues. FC-007A records the disclosure requirement; it introduces no acceptance receipt or parent action. A partial result may be contract-ready, but later service-level enforcement must validate assessment origin, current revisions, disclosure/acceptance and suitability before submission or practice. The existing raw submission path is unchanged and does not yet enforce this gate. No caller should integrate these contracts into production intake before that boundary is implemented.

Scanned text is inert data. Preserve malicious instruction strings as evidence; do not execute them or interpret them as commands, capabilities, approval receipts or assessment metadata. Reject unknown envelope/candidate/assessment properties. A malicious producer can still lie using allowed values: schema validation is not semantic verification or prompt-injection prevention for a future model.

## Deferred decisions (no implicit policy defaults)

- Confidence calibration, trusted assessment production, safety taxonomy/detection and human override rules.
- How partial-set acceptance is recorded and reconciled with detailed approval's existing known-gap checks, without duplicate parent approval workflows or extra routine preparation.
- Capture/image formats, resource limits, permissions, retention, deletion, backups and privacy notices; decide in the capture increment before retaining images.
- Future cloud assistance requires separate privacy, security, cost and implementation approval. No network processing, account or cloud dependency is authorized here.
- Future association verification for visual layouts and handwriting; contract evidence grouping does not prove spatial correctness.

## Consequences

Local-first pure contracts and synthetic fixtures can be tested independently of camera/OCR/storage/UI. No assignment migration, authorization change or dependency is needed. Unsupported and unsafe content cannot be made safe by parent risk acknowledgement alone in the future gate. Existing household policy, immutable sources, selective corrections, development sample restrictions, randomized practice and two-attempt semantics remain authoritative. Privacy and retention remain unresolved, not silently implemented.
